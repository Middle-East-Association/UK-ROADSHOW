Nicholas Hopton
Director General
The Middle East Association
MEA

Nicholas Hopton is Director General of the Middle East Association and a former British Ambassador, with a diplomatic career spanning Europe, the Middle East and Latin America. He brings deep regional expertise and decades of public service experience to the Roadshow's leadership.

Alongside his work at the MEA, Nicholas is a published author and commentator, writing on politics, culture and history for publications including the Times Literary Supplement.

His experience spans international trade, commercial advisory, business consultancy and stakeholder engagement, helping businesses identify opportunities and achieve sustainable growth in overseas markets.

Adam Hosier

Chris Weeks
Speaker
Smart Currency Business
Smart Currency

Over a near 15-year career in risk management, Chris has helped protect British businesses through periods of significant uncertainty, from Brexit and Covid-19 to the current energy crisis. With GCC currencies pegged to the US Dollar, UK businesses trading across the Middle East carry a direct GBP/USD exposure that can quietly erode margins.

Chris works closely with Smart Currency Business's Middle East-facing clients to identify and reduce this risk. He is qualified with an Investment Advice Diploma and licensed to offer regulated advice to UK corporates.

About Smart Currency Business

The UK's largest privately-owned currency provider, with over 20 years' experience. As the official treasury management partner to the MEA, their regional specialists draw on deep knowledge of the GCC and wider MENA region's currency dynamics and payment complexities.

Stuart Carroll
Senior Advisor
Global Diplomacy Institute
GDI · Newcastle

Stuart Carroll is a senior health economist and epidemiologist with over 23 years' experience spanning infectious diseases and vaccines, market access, health technology appraisal, public health and government policy. He recently served as Director of Market Access and Policy Affairs for Moderna UK & Ireland, and previously advised HM Government as a Senior Policy and Strategy Adviser to the UK Vaccines Taskforce, helping negotiate the UK's Strategic Partnership for pandemic preparedness.

Alison Raw
Senior Advisor
Global Diplomacy Institute
GDI · Manchester

Alison Raw joins the MEA Roadshow panel in Manchester as a Senior Advisor at the Global Diplomacy Institute (GDI). A senior strategic leader, international convener and systems thinker, she brings almost 30 years' experience across government, diplomacy, global health and international development, having held senior leadership roles in both the UK Government and the Commonwealth Secretariat — including as Deputy Chief of Staff to the Commonwealth Secretary-General.

She has represented the UK and Commonwealth in high-level international forums and negotiations including the G7, G20, World Health Assembly and UNFCCC COP, working with heads of state, investors, international financial institutions and civil society to connect policy, investment and implementation. Her particular interest is in how governments, businesses and international partners advance long-term national transformation, economic diversification and sustainable growth. Alison is an Honorary Lecturer at the University of Edinburgh and an Invited Professor at the National University of Singapore, and is currently completing a PhD in International Relations.
